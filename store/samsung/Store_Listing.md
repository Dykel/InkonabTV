## Basic information

- **App title:** InkonabTV
- **Category:** Video
- **Age rating:** the app itself has no content; the playlists users add may include any content. Choose the rating Seller Office suggests for a video player that shows user-provided content, with parental control (PIN) available.
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://dykel.github.io/InkonabTV/privacy.html
- **Support:** https://github.com/Dykel/InkonabTV/issues
- **Test account (Account / Password field):** no account needed. On the sign-in screen choose "M3U playlist" and enter `https://dykel.github.io/InkonabTV/test/samsung-review.m3u` (see App_Description.md §3).

## Short description

> A fast, remote-friendly player for your own IPTV playlists (Xtream Codes and M3U).

## Description (English)

> InkonabTV plays the IPTV playlist you already have from your provider, on the big screen.
>
> • Add your playlist with an Xtream Codes account or an M3U link, or set it up from your phone by scanning a code on the TV.
> • Live TV with groups, favorites, recently watched channels, channel numbers and quick channel switching.
> • A full-week TV guide and catch-up replays, when your provider supplies them.
> • Movies and series with posters, summaries, cast and trailers, resume where you stopped, and the next episode plays automatically.
> • My List, search across everything, and a parental PIN for adult categories.
> • Plays HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4 and MKV with Samsung's native player.
> • Available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.
>
> InkonabTV is a media player only. It doesn't include, provide or sell any channels or content. You need a playlist from your own provider to watch anything.
