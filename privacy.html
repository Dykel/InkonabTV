InkonabTV privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Applies to the InkonabTV apps for Samsung TV, Android and the web.

What InkonabTV is

InkonabTV is a media player. It does not provide, host or sell any channels, films, series or other content. You add a playlist from your own provider: an Xtream Codes account (server address, username and password) or an M3U playlist link.

What stays on your device

  • The playlist details you enter (server address, username, password or M3U link), if you choose “Keep me signed in”.
  • A saved copy of your playlist, so the app opens quickly.
  • Your favorites, My List, Continue Watching positions, recently watched channels, settings and the parental PIN.

This information is stored only on your TV, phone or browser. We don’t run accounts, and none of it is sent to us. Signing out deletes it from the app.

Services the app connects to

  • Your playlist provider. The app connects to the server or link you entered to load your playlist and play its streams. That provider’s own privacy policy applies.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB). To show posters, summaries and cast, the app asks TMDB about titles in your playlist by their TMDB number. No personal information is sent. See TMDB’s privacy policy.
  • Remote setup (optional). If you use “Set up from phone”, the playlist details typed on the setup page pass through our pairing service to the TV showing the code. They are kept only until that TV receives them and are deleted automatically, at the latest after 15 minutes. They are never stored afterwards or used for anything else.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

Children

Adult categories from a playlist are hidden by default. Showing them needs a parental PIN, and they are hidden again every time the app starts.
